
# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
## Dairy Website – Daily Sales & Sales Management System

---

## 1. Product Overview

### Product Name
FreshFromFarm – Dairy Sales & Management System

### Product Vision
To digitize and simplify daily dairy operations by enabling easy tracking of milk sales, customers, payments, and inventory through a simple, reliable web platform.

### Problem Statement
Traditional dairy businesses rely on notebooks or Excel sheets to manage daily sales, subscriptions, and payments, which leads to errors, inefficiency, and lack of insights.

### Solution
A web-based dairy management system that helps dairy owners track daily milk sales, manage customers, monitor payments, and view reports.

---

## 2. Goals & Objectives
- Digitize daily dairy sales
- Reduce manual work
- Improve billing accuracy

### Success Metrics
- 100% daily sales logged digitally
- Reduced payment errors
- Faster report generation

## 4. User Personas

### Delivery Staff

---

## 5. Key Features

### User Authentication
- Login / Logout
- Role-based access

### Customer Management
- Add, edit, delete customers
- Milk type, rate, and address tracking

### Daily Milk Sales
- Date-wise sales entry
- Auto amount calculation

### Subscription Management
- Daily / alternate-day subscriptions
- Pause / resume delivery

### Payment Management
- Cash, UPI, bank transfer
- Pending dues tracking

### Inventory Management
- Daily stock entry
- Sold vs remaining milk

### Reports & Analytics
- Daily & monthly reports
- Customer-wise reports
- Export PDF / Excel

### Dashboard
- Total milk sold today
- Today’s revenue
- Pending payments
